Our Philosophy

PATTERN:LAB was created by Naveena Ayeshmantha for helps and enhance the talent and students who have creative vision. Our team is a community driven by precision, innovation, and the desire to empower the next generation of designers.

Our resources are curated to simplify complex grading, fit, and textile challenges, turning the meticulous art of pattern making into an inspiring phase of the design process.
